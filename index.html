Privacy Policy – Calmarest Last updated: November 22, 2026 Calmarest ("the app") is developed by Freegesh Apps. The app provides guided audio sessions for relaxation, focus, sleep and emotional release. This policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled. 1. Information we collect The app does not collect, store on any server or share any personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, and no analytics or tracking. 2. Information stored only on your device To work properly, the app saves a few settings locally on your device: Your chosen language and preferred session type Your sleep timer choice and reminder settings Your progress, such as completed sessions, minutes and day streak This information never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data. 3. Notifications If you allow notifications, the app shows gentle reminders, at most two per day. They are created on your device. You can turn them off at any time in the app settings or in your phone settings. 4. Background audio The app uses a media playback service so that sessions keep playing when the screen is off. It shows a playback notification with controls while audio is playing. Audio files are included in the app and no internet connection is needed. 5. Ratings The app may ask you to rate it using Google Play's in-app review feature. This is handled by Google Play under Google's Privacy Policy. The app does not receive your review details. 6. Advertising The current version of the app does not show ads. If ads are added in a future version, this policy will be updated before that version is released to explain what data the advertising provider uses. 7. Children The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from children. 8. Changes to this policy We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. 9.
